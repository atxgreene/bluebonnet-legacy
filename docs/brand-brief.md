# Bluebonnet Legacy Website Improvements

## Core positioning
Bluebonnet Legacy should lead with one clear promise: helping founder-led and family-owned businesses convert decision latency into financial clarity, operational structure, and transition readiness.

## What changed from the mockups
- Replaced placeholder/gibberish text with actual executive-facing copy.
- Kept the premium Texas/institutional feel while making the page usable as a live homepage.
- Elevated the Bluebonnet Alignment Framework™ into the central visual system.
- Moved the site toward one primary conversion action: Request a Diagnostic.
- Added a service architecture that can scale into advisory, operating cadence, transition readiness, and technology leverage.
